Questions about the Screen

Before the callback.

Can a firm keep its existing contact form?

The contact-form journey shows how a firm can reply by email with a link to the Screen. The name, contact details, and original message appear in the follow-up. The handoff depends on how the firm’s form and replies are connected.

What if someone calls instead of writing?

The phone journey shows two routes. The prospective consultee can answer the AI intake assistant by voice, or the firm can send a text link after a missed call. In the second route, the person describes the matter after opening the link.

What happens when information is missing or unclear?

A missing answer stays open. An ambiguous answer becomes a question for the lawyer to confirm. A failed retrieval is recorded as a technical issue and does not trigger a rejection.

What goes into the complete report?

The report depends on the practice area and the information supplied. It can include the submitted facts, their sources, points that need attention, and questions for review. Family enquiries use an intake-only report.

Who decides whether to offer a consultation?

The lawyer reviews the enquiry, checks conflicts and capacity, and decides how the firm responds. Submitting information does not establish representation.
