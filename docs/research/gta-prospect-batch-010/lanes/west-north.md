## Method

Search was used only to discover candidate firms. Each recorded evidence URL is a public firm-owned roster, people, practice, contact-location, or homepage page. Before retaining a source, I checked the site's public `robots.txt` where available and reviewed public terms/disclaimer availability; I did not automate or access the LSO, submit a form, open chat, use booking, call/email, inspect GBP or advertising, or copy contact data.

`exact` means the reviewed first-party page has a bounded lawyer roster that distinguishes non-lawyer roles. `at_least` means the evidence supports the stated minimum but not the complete firm roster. `uncertain` is never promoted as a candidate. Counts are point-in-time observations, not licensing, firm-entity, marketing, or outreach findings.
