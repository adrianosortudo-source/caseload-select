# Downtown Toronto 1–10 lawyer cohort — batch D

Research date: 2026-09-08  
Method: public, read-only review of first-party pages and limited public corroboration. No form, chat, phone, email, or booking interaction was performed.

## Decision rules used

`Eligible` means the current public roster supports one to ten practising lawyers and the office is within the downtown Toronto cohort. A firm is *not* fully qualified for outreach from this batch unless there is support for its location, roster, owner/leadership, direct owner/leader email, observable advertising activity, GBP finding, website finding, and visible intake channels. Missing evidence is recorded as unknown; it is not treated as a negative finding.

For geographic review, plainly North York / North Toronto office addresses are excluded conservatively. The remaining addresses are treated as downtown Toronto candidates pending the cohort's central boundary reconciliation.

`No observed advertising activity` means this review did not locate a dated ad-library record, sponsored placement, or other direct advertising evidence. It does **not** mean the firm never advertises or has not spent on advertising.
