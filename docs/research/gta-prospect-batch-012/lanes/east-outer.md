## Scope and method

This bounded source queue covers private-practice firms with a public first-party presence in Durham/east and approved outer-GTA municipalities. Discovery used public search. Evidence was retained only from accessible firm-owned roster, team, about, office, or contact pages. Counts are visible current lawyer roles; support staff, clerks, articling students, and retired lawyers are excluded. `exact` means the cited page exposed a bounded current roster; `at_least` would mean a defensible minimum, though this lane's six promoted source-queue candidates are exact.

No forms, chats, logins, outreach, ads, GBP, CRM, database, production import, or anti-bot bypass was used. Every record remains `accepted: false` and `import_ready: false` pending independent review and central reconciliation. Published email evidence is retained only when the value was visibly present on a cited first-party page; no email pattern was inferred. Relationship types are recorded only where the cited page used an explicit title; otherwise they remain `unknown`.
