## Evidence handling notes

- Direct email means the address was published in a first-party profile or firm-controlled page. It has not been deliverability-tested and must not be used to send outreach from this research process.
- No forms, chat widgets, booking tools or phone numbers were used. “Visible intake” records only publicly presented channels.
- `bad GBP` and `bad website` require a specific current, reviewable observation. This batch preserves provisional signals but does not elevate them to those findings.
- Suggested next evidence work: official Downtown Plan address overlay for Sokoloff and Gottlieb; direct Google Maps capture for all eligible firms; browser/mobile review of the current inquiry flow; and ad-library/sponsored-search checks for the eligible firms with incomplete advertising evidence.
