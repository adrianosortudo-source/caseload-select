# Downtown Toronto cohort — batch A research

Observation date: 2026-09-08 (America/Toronto). This is a public, read-only research record. No forms, chat widgets, telephone numbers, or email addresses were used to contact a firm.

## Decision rules used in this batch

- `downtown: confirmed` means the stated office is plainly within the financial district / core downtown. `boundary review` means the address must be checked against the cohort's adopted downtown boundary. `outside` means the stated office is plainly not downtown.
- Lawyer counts are current public-roster counts, excluding staff, clerks, and students. They are not Law Society licence verification.
- A direct owner email must be published by the firm in connection with that person. A general inbox is recorded separately and does not satisfy the owner-email requirement.
- Ads are only `observed` when a public ad, sponsored placement, or ad-library record is captured. Pixels / analytics tags are technical signals only.
