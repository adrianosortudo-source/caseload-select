# Downtown Toronto cohort — research batch F

**Research date:** 2026-09-08  
**Method:** public, read-only review of first-party pages and limited corroborating public directories/search results. No forms were submitted, chat initiated, or firm contacted.  
**Decision rule:** a record can only be marked `fully qualified` after firm identity, a downtown office, current 1–10 lawyer count, owner/leader and a public direct business email, observable advertising activity, a supported GBP opportunity, a supported website opportunity, and visible intake routes are all evidenced. A tag-manager or analytics tag alone is not advertising activity.
